## Troubleshooting done so far (by support)

1. The original gateway (`0010EC022241`) was checked and rebooted on site. It connected for a short time, then dropped offline again.
2. It was swapped for `0010EC0221F0`. The replacement did exactly the same thing.
3. The original gateway was tested at the branch and worked normally.
   Support's conclusion: the cause is the site, not the gateway hardware.

## Initial assessment (unconfirmed, no logs yet)

1. **It is not clear what "goes offline" means.** It could be the gateway losing its cellular link to ACS,
   or the remotes dropping off the Dust mesh. Either one shows up in ACS as a missed reading.
2. **Most likely cause: the same failure as `../2026-10-19_socket-errors/`.**
   If the cellular link at the site is weak or flaky, the gateway hits a chain of errors:
   `Sock Recv ERROR` → `Too many failed sockets` → `SW Reseting... Type=10`.
   Each of those app restarts takes down the Dust network, so the remotes drop and have to power up and rejoin again.
   That matches "connects, then quickly goes offline", and both units behaving the same way.
   It also explains why the unit worked at the branch, where coverage is better.
   `../2026-11-11_reboot_no_mote_conn/` turned out to be this exact failure.
3. **If the uplink is fine, look at the mesh.** Possible causes are the repeater path to the remotes in the classified area,
   or RF interference. In that case the gateway would stay connected to ACS while individual remotes go missing.
4. **The ACS list suggests there are two separate problems** (see below):
   - 17 motes went quiet together on 11/17. That matches the gateway or uplink dropping out.
   - 3 motes have been silent since 09/30. That looks like a mesh or placement problem.

Observations:

1. **There are three groups of motes:**
   - **17 motes last reported on 11/17, all within 9 minutes (10:13 to 10:22 PM).**
     Every one of them stopped at about the same time, and all are now in `Missed Readings`.
   - **3 motes (#11 `9C:3E`, #13 `9C:4B`, #18 `9D:F3`) last reported on 09/30 at exactly 03:27 PM.**
     All three stopped in the same minute, about seven weeks ago.
   - **1 mote (#17 `9D:7A`) reported this morning, 11/18 at 10:10 AM.** It is in `Reorder` (880 psig), not `Missed Readings`.
2. **The 09/30 motes stopped together, so it was one event, not three failures.** Battery is not the cause
   (#18 had 3.48 V on its last reading). Something on 09/30 at about 3:27 PM cut all three off,
   and they never rejoined, even though the other motes kept reporting until 11/17. That makes these three a mesh or placement problem
   rather than an uplink problem. It is worth checking whether they are the classified-area remotes (Location 4) behind the repeater,
   or whether the repeater itself is one of them. It is also worth finding out what happened on site on 09/30:
   the first gateway swap, a repeater move or power loss, or cylinders being moved.
3. **#17 reporting at 10:10 AM today shows the gateway was online and passing data this morning.** That doesn't fit
   "the gateway has been offline since 11/17 10:22 PM". Possible explanations:
   - The gateway is up but the mesh is mostly down. Only #17 is still joined, or it was the first to rejoin after an app restart.
   - The gateway connects and drops repeatedly (the "connects, then quickly goes offline" symptom). #17 happened to report
     during a short window when the link was up.
   - #17's report was sent right away because of the threshold crossing (`Reorder`), so it got through during that window
     while the other motes' scheduled readings were missed.
   Checking ACS history for `9D:7A` around 10:10 AM, and whether any other mote reported in the same window, would tell these apart.
4. **#12 `9C:48` ("Other", 0 psig, no AirgasCustID or TankCode) looks like a spare or placeholder mote.** It still reports.
   #13 `9C:4B` has no Dispatcher set. Neither matters for this bug, but both are worth tidying in ACS.
5. **`DaysToEmpty` is -41551.0 on every mote,** which is obviously wrong. That is an unrelated ACS display issue.
6. **#1 `00:17:0D:00:00:74:00:B4` is from a different MAC range** from the other 19 (`…75:xx:xx`), so it may be an older mote.

| 1810257 | `3db2da257bb44cc3b599bcb687e214a9` | RSSI: oFono `NetworkRegistration` `Strength` / `Technology` / `Status` |

Collect with `python3 mgwctl.py status <job ID> --insecure`. To convert oFono `Strength` to raw values:
CSQ ≈ Strength × 31 / 100, and dBm = −113 + 2 × CSQ. When and whether these jobs get picked up also shows when the gateway's uplink is working.

**Version numbering:** RPM reports versions like `concentrator-1.1.7-r19` or `1.2.0-4`, while this ticket and
the socket-errors issue use `V 1.01.03` / `V 1.01.07`. Confirm how the two map before deciding
whether the unit has the fix (`1.1.7` is probably `1.01.07`).
